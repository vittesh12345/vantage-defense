Capabilities

What it did, why, and whether it's a threat.

Vantage answers behavioral questions, not positional ones. One stack covers maneuver, intent, and proximity threat, running where the mission is, including fully offline on your own hardware.

    Intent classification

    Names the reason for a burn (station-keeping, orbit raise or lower, rendezvous, collision-avoidance) with a confidence band and a rule-based, fully auditable rationale on every call. No black box to trust.

  3. 03

    Proximity and RPO watch

    Separates a deliberate shadower from an incidental pass or a benign co-orbiting formation. One-sidedness is the discriminator.

    Benign formation

    Two nodes in the same formation hold their spacing because orbital mechanics puts them there. Neither one spends propellant to stay.

    Shadowing

    One party maneuvers to hold station next to another that never agreed to it. That asymmetry is what separates a shadower from a neighbor.

    100%on seeded shadowing
  4. 04

    Collision risk screening prior

    Conjunction screening calibrated against 162,634 ESA close-approach reports. Public element sets carry no covariance, so this is a labeled screening prior: it ranks which conjunctions deserve an analyst, not an operational probability of collision.

Platform

From a new element set to an assessed event.

The why comes off a pipeline that runs end-to-end in seconds, turning a public catalog into an assessed event.

01 · Ingest

Public catalogs in

Open connectors normalize public catalogs (CelesTrak, Space-Track) and five-year element-set histories into one data fabric. No proprietary sensor required.

02 · Detect

Subtract physics

Remove J2 oblateness and atmospheric drag; the residual is thrust, detected, measured, timestamped. 519 maneuvers on a 1,039-object catalog in 6.0 s.

03 · Assess

Name the behavior

Six models name intent, score conjunction and shadowing risk, and check each object against its own pattern of life.

04 · Report

Into the analyst queue

The event arrives with the signals behind it attached, ranked so the calls that need a human get one first.
