      About this visualization

      An interactive map of the low Earth orbit population: ~20,300 payloads, rocket bodies and debris fragments, propagated with a two-body + J2 model and colored by object type. The population is a deterministic snapshot shaped like the real catalog — constellation shells, the documented debris clouds and well-known spacecraft at their published elements.

      Getting around
      • Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · click an object to inspect it
      • Search by name or catalog ID; filter by type, altitude, country or constellation
      • Conjunctions — pick an event to jump the clock to closest approach with both orbits drawn
      • Ground view — choose a sensor site (or click its label) to stand on the ground and watch passes overhead; drag to look around, scroll to zoom
      • Time — play/pause, 1×–1000×, scrub ±24 h, jump to a date, or return to LIVE
      • Keys: Space play/pause · L live · Esc clear selection
      Legend
      • Payloads — operational and derelict spacecraft
      • Rocket bodies — spent upper stages
      • Debris — fragmentation and collision fragments
      • Unknown — uncorrelated tracks
